A NOTE ON PRIVACY

Know how your room works.

We want the rules to be clear. Here are a few important things to know before sharing a room.

Your link is your invitation

Anyone who has the room link may be able to enter. Share it only with people you trust.

No sign-up required

You can join with a display name; the current chat does not require a user account.

Temporary room history

Messages are kept in server memory while the room is active. When everyone leaves, the room and its history are removed.

A practical reminder

Avoid posting passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information. This is not an end-to-end encrypted service.
